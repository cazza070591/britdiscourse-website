Welcome to BritDiscourse

My name is Daniel Carrington. Having lived in Poland for over 7 years and bringing extensive experience from corporate backgrounds, I possess a comprehensive understanding of the precise communication barriers Polish professionals encounter when engaging in English. By witnessing firsthand the transition from overanalyzing grammatical structures to experiencing hesitation in live discussions, I established BritDiscourse to cultivate authentic, real-world conversational fluency and significantly elevate professional confidence.
