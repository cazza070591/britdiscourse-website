Welcome to BritDiscourse

My name is Daniel Carrington. I have lived in Poland for over 7 years and bringing extensive experience from corporate backgrounds, I possess a comprehensive understanding of the precise communication barriers Polish professionals encounter when engaging in English. By witnessing firsthand the transition from overanalyzing grammatical structures to experiencing hesitation in live discussions, I established BritDiscourse to cultivate authentic, real-world conversational fluency and significantly elevate professional confidence. I will help people of all ages from kids, teenagers, and adults, as I am here to help you take your English to the next level.

💡 Note on Online Sessions: All virtual lessons are conducted smoothly via Google Meet or Microsoft Teams, allowing screen sharing, digital notes, and instant feedback.
