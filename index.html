Welcome to BritDiscourse

My name is Daniel Carrington. I have lived in Poland for over 7 years and bring extensive experience from corporate backgrounds, including working as a recruiter where I conducted over 50 interviews for various positions. Because of this background, I possess an insider’s perspective on how interviews are conducted, what interviewers look for, and what they seek to avoid. Combined with a comprehensive understanding of the precise communication barriers Polish professionals encounter when engaging in English—moving from overanalyzing grammatical structures to experiencing hesitation in live discussions—I established BritDiscourse to cultivate authentic, real-world conversational fluency and significantly elevate professional confidence. I help people of all ages from kids, teenagers, and adults, and I am here to help you take your English to the next level.

💡 Note on Online Sessions: All virtual lessons are conducted smoothly via Google Meet or Microsoft Teams, allowing screen sharing, digital notes, and instant feedback.

Privacy Policy, Roles & Legal Compliance

Welcome to BritDiscourse. This Privacy Policy outlines data handling practices pursuant to Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (GDPR / RODO) and the Polish Personal Data Protection Act of 10 May 2018 (Ustawa o ochronie danych osobowych).

1. Legal Roles & GDPR Article 4(7) Governance

Pursuant to Article 4(7) of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), Daniel Carrington acts as the Data Controller (Administrator Danych Osobowych) determining the purposes and means of processing personal data. Third-party providers integrated into this platform—specifically Calendly (for appointment scheduling under GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), Web3Forms (for contact form data routing), PayU / Przelewy24 (for financial processing under Polish Accounting Act / Ustawa o rachunkowości), Fakturownia (for automated invoicing), and Google Meet/Microsoft Teams (for interactive virtual coaching sessions)—act as independent Data Controllers or Data Processors.

2. Information Collected & Legal Basis (GDPR Article 6)

In accordance with GDPR Article 6(1)(a) (consent) and Article 6(1)(b) (performance of a contract), we collect basic information provided voluntarily, including full names, email addresses, and scheduling inquiries submitted via Calendly and contact forms.

3. Data Usage, Retention & Polish Accounting Law

Data is processed strictly to provide coaching services and issue automated invoices/rachunki via Fakturownia pursuant to the Polish Accounting Act of 1994 (Ustawa o rachunkowości), which mandates financial document retention for 5 years. Transaction data via PayU / Przelewy24 and scheduling data via Calendly are stored securely in compliance with respective legal retention schedules.

4. Web3Forms & Calendly Disclaimer & Security

In compliance with technical security mandates under GDPR Article 32, contact messages transmitted through Web3Forms and appointments coordinated through Calendly use encrypted transmission protocols (SSL/TLS). Data is never commercialized or transferred to unauthorized third parties.

5. User Rights under GDPR Chapter III (Articles 15–22)

Under Chapter III of the GDPR (Articles 15–22), data subjects have the absolute right to access, rectify, erase ('right to be forgotten'), restrict processing, and data portability regarding their personal information stored by Daniel Carrington.

6. Lesson Flexibility & Rescheduling Terms

In accordance with agreed mutual service terms, scheduling is fully flexible via Calendly, provided any session adjustments or cancellations occur at least 24 hours prior to the reserved slot.

7. Supervisory Authority (UODO)

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, specifically the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych - UODO) in Poland, if you consider that the processing of your personal data infringes GDPR.
